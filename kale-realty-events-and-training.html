Why It Matters

WHY IN-PERSON TRAINING AND COMMUNITY MATTER

Online training has real value. But some things — trust, accountability, energy, and the kind of learning that happens when experienced agents work alongside newer ones — don't fully translate through a screen.

🤝
Relationships you can actually use
In-person events create the kind of connections that lead to referrals, co-listings, mentorship conversations, and the informal support that helps agents solve problems in real time.
🎙️
Learning that sticks differently
A live presentation, a group role-play, or a Q&A with an experienced agent in the room produces a different kind of retention than a recorded module. Both matter. Neither replaces the other.
🌆
Chicago is a local business
Real estate is fundamentally local. Events rooted in Chicago — and in the neighborhoods where agents actually work — build the market knowledge and community presence that help agents serve clients well.

Common Questions

FREQUENTLY ASKED.

What types of events does Kale Realty host for agents?
Kale hosts a mix of events throughout the year — quarterly investor summits, live training sessions, community service outings, and onboarding events. In-person events are included for Kale agents as part of membership, with no separate registration fee.
What is Share Our Spare?
Share Our Spare is a Chicago community organization that collects donated household goods and furniture and distributes them to families in need. Kale Realty agents participated as volunteers — the photos on this page are from that event.
What happens at the Kale Investor Summit?
The Investor Summit brings together Kale agents and Chicago real estate investors for education, market strategy, and networking. Kale has hosted both a Summer and a Fall edition. The agenda typically covers deal analysis, investor relationships, and market conditions.
What was the Garfield Park outing?
The Garfield Park outing was a team community day where Kale agents volunteered together in Chicago's Garfield Park neighborhood. It's one example of how Kale involves agents in the broader Chicago community — not just in transactions.
Are Kale events open to agents at other brokerages?
Some events are Kale-member events. Others may have broader community or investor involvement. If you're interested in attending or learning more about upcoming events, reach out at JoinKale.com or call 312-939-5253.
How does Kale Realty support agents beyond online training?
Beyond recorded and online resources, Kale offers quarterly in-person summits, daily live coaching sessions, a 25-person in-house mentor network, Kaley A.I., and community events. The in-person layer is a deliberate part of how Kale structures agent development — not an afterthought. Learn more about the full training program at KaleTraining.com.
How do I learn more or see if Kale Realty is right for me?
Schedule a no-pressure call at JoinKale.com. You can also read the Illinois real estate licensing guide if you're earlier in the process of getting licensed. Kale is based in Chicago at 3323 W Diversey Ave — 312-939-5253.
