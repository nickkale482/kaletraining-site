Agent → How do I handle a low appraisal on my listing?
Great question. First, request a copy of the appraisal report and review the comps used. If the comps are weak, pull 3 stronger ones from the last 90 days within a half mile and submit a formal rebuttal to the lender...
Agent → What's a good script for expired listings?
Here's a proven opener: "Hi, this is [name] with Kale Realty. I noticed your home came off the market recently and I wanted to reach out because I've helped 4 sellers in your neighborhood successfully close this year..."
Agent → How do I close a buyer consultation?
End every buyer consult with a commitment question, not a yes/no. Try: "Based on what we discussed today, are you comfortable moving forward with me as your agent?" Then be quiet.
